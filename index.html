Our Love Story

A few moments along the way

2019

Where It All Began

Our story began in the simplest and most unexpected way—with a college project and an email account.

We first got to know each other through a common friend who was close to both of us. An instructor required a project to be submitted through email, but one of us did not have an email account yet. With a little help from our common friend, an account was created—and somehow, that simple moment became the beginning of our story.

As we exchanged the details needed for the account, we also started getting to know each other. Soon, simple conversations turned into meaningful ones, and we began sharing more moments together.

2019

January 25, 2019 — The Sweetest "Yes"

Then came the moment that changed everything.

On January 25, 2019, at exactly 10:22 AM, a sweet "Yes" was received through a text message.

Later that same day, we met at school and confirmed that yes in person. There were smiles, a little happiness, and a moment that quietly marked the beginning of our journey together. And just like that, we went back to our classes—perhaps unaware that such a simple moment would become one of the most important memories of our lives.

We would sometimes walk together toward the gate after class before going our separate ways. They were ordinary moments at the time, but they eventually became memories we would always treasure.

2019–2025

The Years That Followed

Through the years, we celebrated anniversaries, created countless memories, and found a special place where we loved to visit and pray—God the Father in Fatima, Inabanga.

Our journey was not always easy. We experienced distance, different schedules, school and work responsibilities, and eventually the challenges brought by the COVID-19 pandemic.

There were disagreements, arguments, misunderstandings, and moments when we almost gave up. But through every challenge, we continued to choose each other, learn, forgive, and grow together.

2025

December 8, 2025 — The Engagement

After years of memories, challenges, prayers, and dreams for the future, we got engaged on December 8, 2025.

We started preparing for this next chapter with what we had—our small savings and our humble salaries. Little by little, we saved, sacrificed, and worked toward the wedding we had been praying for.

2026

Our Forever Begins

As we prepare to celebrate our wedding on November 28, 2026, we look back with grateful hearts at how far we have come.

Our story has never been perfect, but it has always been ours. We learned that love is not only found in happy moments, but also in choosing to stay, understand, forgive, pray, and grow together.

With God at the center of our plans, we are ready to begin the next chapter of our lives together.

From a simple email account in 2019, to a sweet "Yes" at 10:22 AM, to a lifetime together in 2026—this is our story, and this is only the beginning of our forever.
